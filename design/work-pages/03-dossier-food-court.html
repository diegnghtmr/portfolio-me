01

One order, three kinds of work

An order starts with a customer’s menu choice and then becomes work for staff. Each role needs a different view, while the order must keep a trace of every state change.

Customers
Browse menus and place orders.
Restaurant owners
Manage restaurants and dishes.
Employees
Move orders through states.

03

System map

One web interface serves three roles. Four Spring Boot services separate who can act, what’s on the menu, what state an order is in, and how it got there.

  • order-microservice ↗

  • traceability-microservice ↗

04

Decisions & trade-offs

The repository boundaries are visible. The reasons for choosing them still need confirmation.

Separate services or one API?

Draft — to confirm
Options considered
One API with internal modules, or one service per responsibility.
Choice
Separate identity, menu, order, and traceability services.
Consequence
Each capability has a clear home, but behavior that crosses services needs explicit contracts.

Order history as a separate concern

Draft — to confirm
Options considered
An audit table inside the order service, or a dedicated history service.
Choice
Give order history its own service.
Consequence
History has a clear owner, but the handoff from a state change to its record needs verification.
