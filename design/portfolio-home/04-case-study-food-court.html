    Problem

    Draft inferred from the repositories. Confirm in M1.

    A food court runs many restaurants behind one counter. Owners, employees and customers need different views of the same orders, and nobody should wait for a monolith to redeploy when the menu changes.

    Decisions

    Draft from the repositories' descriptions. Final wording comes from the code and ADRs in M1.

    One service per business capability

    Chosen: identity, menu, order and traceability as separate Spring Boot services. Instead of: one shared backend. Consequence: each can deploy alone; cross-service calls need contracts.

    Traceability as its own service

    Chosen: a dedicated service records the full order history and efficiency reports. Consequence: reporting never slows the order path.
